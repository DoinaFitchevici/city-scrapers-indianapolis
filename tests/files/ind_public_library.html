Library Board Meeting Schedule

The Indianapolis Public Library Board of Trustees meets at 6:30 p.m., typically on the fourth Monday of each month. All board meetings are expected to be held in-person at the Library location specified. Locations may change or be adjusted - check this page for any updates. Public comment can be made in-person at each regular board meeting. Messages can also be submitted to board members online.
